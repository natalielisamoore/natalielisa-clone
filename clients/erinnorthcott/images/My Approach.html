My sessions combine science, spirituality, and practical tools. They are grounded in safety and
structure, with space for the organic and intuitive to thrive. Although there is ample room for
dialogue, this is not talk therapy. Most of the work is done from a deeper more relaxed state
that allows you to access the parts of yourself that hold the wisdom you need the most.


At the end of a session, I will offer optional tools for deeper integration of the work we have
done. The tools are tailored to you and may be listening to a short recording I made for you, a
breath or meditation technique, simple drawing or journaling, or something we come up with
together.

Working together

In many ways working with me is like us forming a team, and giving me the role of “guide to your inner world”.


By creating a safe container, asking deeper questions at certain key moments, and offering the appropriate practices, I work with you to bring the highest version of you forward to lead your journey.


You are always in control, you are the leader, you set the pace. Your inner wisdom is the guiding star for your journey...and like any good guide, I have asked you for all the details about where you want to arrive to, and I have the skills to help you get there.


Three Pillars

Through working with my clients and my own life journey, I have developed the three pillars that I base a lot of my sessions and my own life on.


The pillars are Self-Alignment, Self-Assurance and Self-devotion. I use the word “self” here to represent that the goal here is to be able to process enough in our sessions to then learn the tools to be able to make these three pillars a natural part of how you self-regulate, and live.

Modalities

I guarantee a safe grounded space for you online or in-person. Some of the methods I use in our sessions draw from my training in Somatic Therapy, Embodiment, Parts Work, Inner Child Work, Hypnotherapy, NLP, and Breath-work. When appropriate I create 5-8 minute “personalized integration recordings” for you to listen to after our sessions, to deepen your work from our sessions. You will always hold the power to choose what methods we use, and the pace that’s right for your process. We are all unique, and so the work we do together is highly personalized to you. I invite you to connect with me for an initial call, at no charge, let’s see if working together feels right for us.

Our Inner World

I see our inner worlds in likeness to how the earth has an incredible variety of terrains. Our inner worlds are diverse, they have weathered storms, over the years some surfaces have become jagged, others smooth. Throughout the terrains there are gentle waters, caves of refuge with shiny crystals inside waiting to be found. All these different elements in your inner world are all “parts” of you. Having your inner parts aligned with what you want takes some focus, and the results are often profound. There are no “bad’ parts of you, there are only parts in need of attention, comfort, empowerment and new information. As each part of you gets the attention it needs, life becomes lighter, a spaciousness develops like a clearing within, and attainment of what you really want becomes not only possible, but completely natural.
