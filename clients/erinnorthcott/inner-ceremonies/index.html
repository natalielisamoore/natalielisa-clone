Thresholds

When life is asking you to change

Standing at a crossing. Maybe it’s a loss. An ending. A beginning. A change the body hasn’t caught up with yet. Maybe life looks fine from the outside, and you can’t remember the last time you felt like yourself.

Relationship patterns are the heart of this work. We feel something so strongly, and then the pattern is there again. Who we choose, why we choose them, what we’re really reaching for.

Should I stay? Should I go? Why does this keep happening?

In ceremony, the pattern reveals itself. Sometimes it takes a single session to see what has been running for a lifetime. Every kind of crossing finds refuge here: grief, new chapters, patterns with money or work, big life choices.

Wherever you’re standing, the work meets you there.

The Work

What happens in an Inner Ceremony

Your body already knows. An Inner Ceremony is a private session held online, from your own home. This is not talk therapy: together we travel past the thinking mind into a deeper, relaxed state, where your body holds what your mind hasn’t found words for yet.

There are no bad parts of you, only parts in need of attention, comfort, and new information. As each part gets what it needs, life becomes lighter. A clearing develops within.

You are always in control. You set the pace. Afterward, I create a personal integration recording: your own wisdom from the session, spoken back to you.

The inner world changes first. The outer world changes after.

Your Guide

Erin Northcott

It’s the thing people say to me again and again, often before they can explain why. Perhaps it’s because my path to this work began the way most true things do: through living it.

I spent eleven years carrying my brother Daniel’s story to the world, producing the HBO documentary of his life and death. Along that road I came to know grief intimately, lost myself, and found my way back: through ceremony, somatic work, and the slow practice of returning to my own body. What I learned became something I could offer others.

Trained in somatic therapy, embodiment, parts work, inner child work, hypnotherapy, NLP, and breathwork, I blend what I’ve learned into something entirely my own: ceremonial in feeling, grounded in the body, and rooted in the bond between people and the earth. I work with clients worldwide from my home on Salt Spring Island, where I live with my son among the forests that shaped me.

A producer, somatic guide, and mother. Everyone is welcome here.
