Inner Ceremonies

A guided journey back to yourself.

The Work

A private, one-to-one somatic session, held online from your own home. We lower the gaze, settle the breath, and travel inward, to where your body holds what your mind has no words for.

Nothing to prepare, nothing to learn. Sit or lie down, eyes closed or open. The session is built around your comfort.

You don’t need to arrive with words. We find them together.

The inner world changes first. The outer world changes after.

Thresholds

People come standing at a threshold: a loss, an ending, a beginning, a change the body hasn’t caught up with yet.

Relationship thresholds are the heart of the work. We feel something so strongly, and then the pattern is there again. It began long before we could see it, and it keeps running until we do.

Should I stay? Should I go? Why does this keep happening?

In ceremony, the pattern reveals itself. Often a single session is enough to see what has been happening all along, and everything about how you choose begins to change.

Whatever you’re moving through, the doorway is the same: becoming solid enough within yourself to hold yourself.

A Session

  1. 01

    Before

    A short conversation. What’s alive for you, and what you’d like to explore.

  2. 02

    During

    We follow what arises: an image, a feeling, a place in the body.

  3. 03

    After

    A personal meditation, recorded for you, so you can return to what you found.

First ceremonies run around two hours. Ongoing sessions are ninety minutes.
