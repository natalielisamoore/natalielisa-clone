Erin — The Work

Erin Northcott, Producer

For eleven years, Erin lived inside her brother Daniel’s story — the footage he filmed across the world, the life he lived without hesitation, and the loss that reshaped her own. That long labor of love became a film, and that film carried Daniel — his life and his death — to HBO, and to millions.

Erin Northcott is a producer, storyteller, and somatic guide whose work lives where story, body, grief, and transformation meet. After eleven years carrying Daniel’s archive into an HBO documentary, her work expanded into Inner Ceremonies: a gentle, body-led practice for people moving through thresholds of their own.
