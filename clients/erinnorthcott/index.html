Reel 01 — The Archive

Daniel

For eleven years, Erin lived inside her brother Daniel’s story — the footage he filmed across the world, the life he lived without hesitation, and the loss that reshaped her own. That long labor of love became a film, and that film carried Daniel — his life and his death — to HBO, and to millions.

Reel 02 — The Becoming

Inner Ceremonies

Somewhere across those eleven years, the work stopped being only about Daniel. Grief became a teacher. The body became a doorway. Out of that long passage a practice was born — Erin’s somatic work for people moving through thresholds of their own.
